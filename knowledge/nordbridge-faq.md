# NordBridge Logistics — FAQ Knowledge Base

> Fictional company for demo purposes. Contact: support@nordbridge-logistics.example.com

**Q1: What does NordBridge Logistics do?**
A1: We are a small freight forwarder specializing in shipments between China and Japan — sea freight, air freight, and door-to-door delivery for small and medium businesses.

**Q2: How do I request a shipping quote?**
A2: Email quotes@nordbridge-logistics.example.com with cargo type, weight, dimensions, pickup city, and delivery city. We reply with a quote within one business day.

**Q3: How long does sea freight from Shanghai to Tokyo take?**
A3: Port-to-port is typically 5–7 days. Door-to-door including customs clearance is 8–12 days.

**Q4: How long does air freight from Shanghai to Osaka take?**
A4: Airport-to-airport is 1–2 days; door-to-door including customs is 3–5 days.

**Q5: What is the minimum chargeable weight?**
A5: Sea freight (LCL): 1 cubic meter minimum. Air freight: 45 kg minimum chargeable weight.

**Q6: Do you handle customs clearance in Japan?**
A6: Yes. We clear customs at Tokyo, Osaka, and Kobe ports/airports as part of our door-to-door service. The consignee must provide a power of attorney and company registration documents on first use.

**Q7: What documents do I need to provide for export from China?**
A7: Commercial invoice, packing list, and for regulated goods the relevant export license. We provide templates after you request a quote.

**Q8: Can you ship lithium batteries?**
A8: Only under strict conditions: UN38.3 test report, MSDS, and dangerous-goods declaration are required. Standalone loose batteries are not accepted; batteries installed in equipment are handled case by case.

**Q9: Do you offer cargo insurance?**
A9: Yes, optional all-risk coverage at 0.3% of declared cargo value, minimum premium 3,000 yen per shipment.

**Q10: How can I track my shipment?**
A10: You receive a tracking number by email once cargo is loaded. Track it on our website's tracking page; sea shipments update at port departure, transshipment, and arrival.

**Q11: What are your payment terms?**
A11: New customers pay 100% in advance by bank transfer. After three completed shipments, monthly billing (net 30) is available.

**Q12: Do you ship to addresses outside major cities in Japan?**
A12: Yes, nationwide delivery via our trucking partners. Remote areas (Hokkaido, Okinawa, mountainous regions) add 2–3 days.

**Q13: What items are prohibited?**
A13: We do not handle: live animals, perishable food without cold chain booking, cash, precious metals, weapons, and counterfeit goods.

**Q14: Can you consolidate multiple suppliers' goods in China?**
A14: Yes. We consolidate at our Shanghai and Shenzhen warehouses; storage is free for the first 7 days, then 500 yen per cubic meter per day.

**Q15: What happens if my cargo is damaged?**
A15: Report within 7 days of delivery with photos and the delivery receipt. If you purchased our cargo insurance, we file the claim for you; otherwise we assist with the carrier claim but compensation follows the carrier's standard liability limits.

**Q16: Do you offer temperature-controlled shipping?**
A16: Only for full-container (FCL) bookings with 14 days advance notice. LCL cold chain is not available.

**Q17: What are your warehouse addresses in China?**
A17: Shanghai: No. 88 Gangxing Road, Pudong. Shenzhen: No. 12 Baoshi Road, Bao'an District. Receiving hours: Monday–Friday, 9:00–17:00 (China time).

**Q18: Can I change the delivery address after shipment?**
A18: Before customs clearance: free of charge. After clearance: a re-delivery fee of 8,000 yen applies within the same metropolitan area.

**Q19: Do you handle Amazon FBA shipments to Japan?**
A19: Yes. We label and palletize to Amazon Japan FC requirements; provide your FBA shipment ID when booking.

**Q20: What is the cut-off time for weekly Shanghai–Tokyo sailing?**
A20: Cargo must arrive at our Shanghai warehouse by Wednesday 17:00 for Friday sailing.

**Q21: Do you provide packaging services?**
A21: Basic export carton and palletizing: 2,000 yen per cubic meter. Wooden crating for fragile goods is quoted separately.

**Q22: How are volumetric weight charges calculated for air freight?**
A22: Chargeable weight is the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight (length × width × height in cm ÷ 6000).

**Q23: Can you issue a certificate of origin?**
A23: Yes, we arrange CO issuance through the local chamber of commerce for 5,000 yen per set; allow 3 business days.

**Q24: What are your business hours?**
A24: Monday–Friday 9:00–18:00 Japan time. Email support@nordbridge-logistics.example.com; urgent issues on weekends: +81-90-0000-0000 (WhatsApp).

**Q25: Do you offer long-term contract rates?**
A25: Yes. Customers shipping at least 10 cubic meters per month qualify for contract rates, reviewed quarterly. Contact sales@nordbridge-logistics.example.com.
